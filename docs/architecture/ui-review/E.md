## What works well
- Every chip in the group pairs an icon with a word (Healthy/Degraded, Active/Revoked, Sealed/Broken, Pass/Fail), so status never rests on colour alone.
- Secret handling reads clearly: "Show key once", the reveal dialog warns "make sure nobody can see your screen", and production delete flows give a reason.
- The form pages (API, agent, invite, billing) use the numbered `FormSection` and pinned `FormActions` consistently, focus the first field on open, and have unsaved-change guards.
- The AI surfaces follow the rules: answers are in `AIPanel` with "AI-assisted" and "Review before you rely on this" written out, and the four-eyes blocking reason is shown in the Raise dialog before any request.
- The Audit log chain status is always visible in the card header, with an icon and text.
- The RoPA register reads well at desktop width: grouped definition lists with the activity id, scope and event count.

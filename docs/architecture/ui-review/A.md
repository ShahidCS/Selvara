## What works well
- Contrast and focus: a solid 2px blue focus ring on every control tried (nav, buttons, dialogs), and modals (shortcuts, notifications) trap focus and restore it on close.
- The notifications slide-over is clear: tabs with counts, grouped "Needs your decision" and "Alerts", a tone chip with label on every item, Mark all read with Undo.
- The sign-in flow is calm and complete: inline email validation with text, a clear demo-code banner on the second step, neutral reset copy, focus moved to each new heading.
- DataTable on desktop: status tabs with counts, filter chips, sortable headers and row selection behave the same across registers.
- Dark mode in the workspace shell is consistent: the rail, cards, chips and charts all re-tint correctly with no light leftovers.
- The vendor portal's step-by-step flow (email, code, second factor, questionnaire, submit, clarification limited to the flagged answers) is easy to follow on desktop.

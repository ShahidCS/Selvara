## What works well
- Record pages (policy, issue, audit, person) share one pattern: back link, title, status and a two-column layout. The `Stepper` shows Done, Current, Overdue and Projected in words.
- The DataTable registers (Policies, Issues, People, Access) give status tabs with counts, filter chips and an empty state with "Clear filters". The Issues KPI strip and "Needs action" pills read at a glance.
- Segregation-of-duties rules are explained in place: the policy workflow note, the "Closing a High issue needs a verifier…" line, the MFA reset effects list and the auditor's read-only notes.
- Dark mode holds up: chips, heat map and steppers keep readable contrast, and none of these modules shows a hard-coded light colour.
- Destructive dialogs (Offboard, MFA reset) name their effects and require reasons. "Not found" record pages explain the problem and link back.
- The heat map has a legend, numbers in cells, an appetite outline and click-to-filter. The Scoring Criteria page documents the method clearly.

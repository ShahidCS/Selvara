## What works well
- Form pages (Register AI system, Add vendor, Import questionnaire, Add answer, Waiver) share the numbered `FormSection` and pinned `FormActions` pattern, so they look like one family in light and dark.
- Dark mode is clean across all six modules. Chips, KPI strips, the incident banner and modals keep their contrast, and nothing hard-codes a light colour.
- Disabled actions explain themselves (`WhyNot`, segregation-of-duties reasons), and the four-eyes copy is consistent across AI, vendors, trust and questionnaires.
- The vendor record's two-column layout (risk and gate, certificates, findings | monitoring, apps, history) scans well, with empty states ("No certificates", "No findings") in place.
- The AppSec control groups are good disclosure widgets: `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, a live count ("9 of 9 shown"), and an empty search state.
- Trust Center access tables mark the NDA and the blocked domain with an icon plus text, and the public request dialog validates the email inline.

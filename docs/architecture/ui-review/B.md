## Cross-module notes (shared kit, affects every module above)
- There is no visual state for `[aria-invalid="true"]` anywhere in `styles/`. Every form in this group relies on red text at the end of the form (B-15, B-28, B-32).
- `.tbl-wrap` draws its right-edge scroll shadow even when the table does not overflow. Controls, Evidence and Frameworks show a faint stripe at the right edge, so a non-scrolling table looks scrollable (styles/portal.css:331-335). Rows also end about 10px short of the card edge.

## What works well
- The record and form pages are consistent: back link, numbered `FormSection`, pinned Cancel and primary buttons, and the unsaved-changes guard ("Stay on this page" / "Discard changes") works.
- Segregation-of-duties reasons are written out next to disabled actions (attestation, accept and reject, approve deletion), not left as silent disabled buttons.
- The DataTable registers in Frameworks, Controls and Evidence are coherent: tabs with counts, filters, sortable headers, pagination and an empty state.
- Charts say their numbers in text: the donut legend shows counts and percentages, the HBarList shows values, and the heatmap shows % in every cell.
- Dark mode is clean across the group. Status chips keep their icons, and the KPI pills read well.
- The keyboard focus ring is clearly visible, for example on Needs Attention items (`ov-focus.png`).

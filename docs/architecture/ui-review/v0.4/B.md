## What works well
- Record and form pages are consistent and predictable: a round back button, a sticky header, numbered `FormSection`s, and an unsaved-changes guard ("Discard unsaved changes?") that catches the browser Back button.
- Segregation of duties is explained in words where it blocks you: "Only the control owner (O. Hart) can change the status", "Segregation of duties: the requester and the control owner cannot approve the exception", and the evidence reviewer rule.
- `DataTable` behaves well from the keyboard and for screen readers: `aria-sort` changes on sort, the pager marks `aria-current`, filter chips open a listbox driven by arrows and Enter, the bulk bar is a toolbar, and Enter on a row opens the record.
- Charts say their numbers in text: donut legends carry counts and percentages, `HBarList` shows the 80% target and "17 more frameworks, 3 at or above target", and the line and bar charts have hidden data tables.
- Mapping is navigable both ways: a matrix cell opens a drawer (focus moves in, Escape returns it to the cell), requirement IDs open the framework page on that requirement, and the drafts-for-compliance-review notes are present on every mapping view.
- The "Earlier edition" banner on a framework page is exemplary regulatory copy: it says which text readiness is measured against and who must update it.
- Destructive actions now confirm or offer Undo: Remove schedule, Remove file and Request deletion confirm; Snooze and the scope change offer Undo.
- At 200% text the Overview KPI strip behaves as designed (labels wrap, pills move under the label), and dark mode is clean with passing contrast throughout.
- AI is labelled honestly: the posture brief says "computed from your records" and "Suggestions follow fixed rules; nothing is changed until you act", and the AI panel says "Review before you rely on this".

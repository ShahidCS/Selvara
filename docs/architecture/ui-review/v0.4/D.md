# Group D findings: external risk, customer trust, workspace settings

Third Parties, Trust Center admin, Questionnaires (and the vendor portal), Commitments, and the Settings group: Integrations, API, AI Assistant, Team & Rules, Billing & Invoices, Audit Logs and Security Controls (AppSec).

Reviewed live at http://localhost:3301 (v0.4.2 demo) as admin (primary). Owner, employee, auditor, vendor, viewer and analyst were used for access and permission checks, vendor for the vendor portal and viewer for the public Trust Center. Every screen was captured at 1280x900 light, 1280x900 dark and 390x844. Further checks: 200% browser text size, a slow network (2 s latency, 50 KB/s), offline after load and keyboard-only use of Change role, the filter listboxes and the dialogs. Paths are relative to front-end/src/. Screenshots are in shots/D/.

Earlier IDs: findings marked fixed in the v0.2 review that have come back say so in the text (E-30, E-38). F-32, an open item, is cited where it is still visible.

## What works well

- **Change role by keyboard.** The `Listbox` follows the pattern. Enter opens it on the current value, Arrow keys, Home and End move (and wrap), Enter picks, the first Escape closes only the list, and the second closes the dialog with focus back on "Change role of Owen Hart". At 200% text size the list scrolls inside the dialog without covering it.
- **The waiver form is the model to copy.** Each refusal sits under its own field, `aria-invalid` is on that field only, and focus moves to it. The vendor portal does the same for missing answers and adds a summary line.
- **Segregation of duties shows on screen.** Your own request shows Approve disabled with "you cannot approve your own request". The Request approval button explains "Awaiting two approvers (Critical tier)". Toasts name who must approve next.
- **Incident mode is clear and safe.** The banner carries the summary, who started it and when, and "notify affected customers by 30 Nov 2026, 14:16". The KPI badge has an icon and text, and Close incident asks for confirmation.
- **Status never rests on colour alone.** Tiers (Critical with alert, Medium with dot), scores (82/100 with "Acceptable", "Watch" or "Poor"), NDA pending, Sealed and Broken, and Verified all pair an icon with a word, in light and dark.
- **Dark mode holds up across the group.** Chips, the incident banner, AI panels, the chain-intact banner and Segmented selections keep their contrast. No light-only colour was found.
- **The audit trail covers itself.** Exporting the log or generating an evidence package adds a sealed entry. Chain status shows in the card header with an icon and an entry count, and Verify chain gives an explicit result.
- **Secrets and URLs are handled with care.** Webhooks accept https only and focus the field. Keys say "shown once" before they are created. A write-scope key waits for a second admin before it has any secret.

## What works well
- Four-eyes rules are explained in plain words at the moment of action. For example, "Write-offs always need a second elevated super admin to approve", the toast names the amount and its USD value, a duplicate request is refused, the requester sees Withdraw instead of Approve, and the approve dialog says who asked and with which ticket.
- The elevation request has exemplary inline validation (red edge, alert icon, exact fix), a clearly labelled "Simulation" step, and an amber session banner with ticket, end time and End elevation on Elevated Access.
- Onboard a customer validates each field inline, marks it invalid, focuses the first error and keeps the summary short. `SelectList` works by keyboard, and Escape closes the list before the dialog.
- Customer search is a proper combobox (aria-activedescendant, visible active option, Escape, "No customer matches "zzqq"."), with Recently viewed chips.
- Destructive confirmations state the consequence and keep a safe default: "Void INV-2026-120?" with "Keep invoice" / "Void invoice", and "Remove Bilal Raza?" with what is retained.
- Audit Logs reads like evidence: an entry drawer with every field and both hashes, chain verification in success green with an honest "Demo seal: not a cryptographic guarantee" note, an exports register, and "append-only" stated in three places.
- Rates show why a rate is held as visible text, mark pending rows with a chip, fold the rules into a disclosure, and hide the action column for support (F-31 holds).
- Dark mode holds across chips, banners, toasts, disabled buttons and native checkboxes, and no route scrolls sideways at 390 (scrollWidth 390 on all 8 routes). At 200% text the KPI labels and nav wrap instead of truncating.
- Status nearly always carries an icon and a word (Paid, Past due, Held for review, Sealed, Reset pending, Written off), and toasts take an explicit tone.
- The visual base is calm and restrained (light display headings, hairline cards, one accent): the right temperament for a platform console. Targeted fixes (E2-1, E2-6, E2-11) would give it its own privileged identity without a rebrand.

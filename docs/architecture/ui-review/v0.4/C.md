## What works well

- Segregation-of-duties rules are explained where they bite. Examples: "Closing a High issue needs a verifier who is not the assignee…", "You cannot decide on your own access" written into the reviewer's own row, the MFA reset dialog's effects list and three identity checks, the offboarding second-approver note, and the risk acceptance authority ladder ("Lowest authority that can accept: Department head").
- Dialogs and listboxes behave well from the keyboard: Add risk takes focus on its first field, Tab stays trapped in the dialog, Escape closes an open listbox before the dialog, and the focus ring on buttons is a clear 2px blue outline. Tab strips move with the arrow keys.
- axe found no colour-contrast or labelling violations on any register, record tab or dialog in this group, in either theme. Dark mode holds up: chips, steppers, AIPanel and dialogs keep their contrast, with no white surfaces left over.
- Record pages share one pattern: round Back button, breadcrumb with the record ID, status chip, two columns, a "Summary, computed from this record" card, and history. The issue stepper says "Overdue" in words, and the risk approval stepper says "Done" and "Current".
- The Import wizard is careful. It auto-matches columns and shows a sample value for each. "Back to columns" keeps the mapping. The check step counts new, update, warnings and errors, and holds updates for a second admin, saying plainly that "No risk has been changed yet". Wrong file type and empty files get precise messages.
- The heat map has complete accessible names on its cells ("Major impact, Possible likelihood, score 12, High, 1 open risks"). The breakdown bars have text legends and aria-labels with the band mix, and the donut legend gives counts and shares.
- AI content is clearly marked: the AIPanel label, the AI mark, "Write it with AI" kept apart from computed facts, and "Mark reviewed" turning into a "Reviewed by Jordan Avery" chip.
- At 390px nothing in this group causes a sideways page scroll (document width = 390 on every screen). Wide tables scroll inside their cards, and the phone tab bar and KPI 2-up grid work.
- Form pages (New audit, New issue, Register AI, Add contractor) put the error under the field, move focus to it, and ask before you leave with unsaved changes.

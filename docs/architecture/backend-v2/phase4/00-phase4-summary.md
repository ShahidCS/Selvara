## 1. Summary

The design is substantially complete and internally consistent after Phase 3. The checklist gives **2 Pass, 18 Partial and 1 Fail**:
- **Pass:** item 1 (tenant data only in its schema; control plane clean; routing fails closed) and item 13 (upload scanning and isolation).
- **Fail:** item 21 (MVP feasibility), because there is no estimate, budget, stop criteria or technical approver.

The Findings Register holds **65 findings: 1 Critical, 9 High, 34 Medium and 21 Low**. Under the Phase 5 rules, any open Critical or High finding means **No-Go**. Section 4 sets out how each Critical and High finding can be closed.

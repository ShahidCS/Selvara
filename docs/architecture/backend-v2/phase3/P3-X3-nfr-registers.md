# Phase 3 review X3: NFRs, growth, infrastructure and cost; consolidated registers; spine v2; verification checklist

Reviewer: P3-X3 (read-only on the repository). Inputs: `phase2/00-phase2-summary.md` (§3 and §8 binding), `00-spine.md`, 2A to 2K, `2B-classification.md`, ADR-001 to ADR-014, Phase 1 logs. Finding prefix: **P3-X3-**.

## 1. Summary

The 2A NFR table is not yet feasible on the 2H infrastructure in three places. (1) After R2-01, every request reads the control plane `cp` without a cache. `cp` therefore carries the request rate of all cells, yet it has no throughput NFR, no connection ceiling and no load test. (2) The key store `ks` (2F F-D-05, ADR-003) has PITR off and daily backups only, all in `R1`, and exports escrow only the DEK. The cell RPO of 5 minutes and the region RPO of 24 hours are therefore not achievable for E2 fields and files: losing `ks` makes them unrecoverable. (3) The new-cell threshold has three values: 300 in 2A and the cost model, 500 in 2J, and 0.6 × K. The catalog sizes behind them also differ (110 or 140 tables per tenant). This report reconciles them into one rule, CR-1: provisional 300, then floor_100(0.6 × K), with 2A's secondary triggers. The 2H cost model is arithmetically correct, but it leaves out `ks`, the second Vercel project (R2-03), KMS multi-Region replica keys, the egress of nightly exports, the perf cell, the secrets manager and the timestamp service. Together these understate cost by about 17% at 10 tenants, 30% at 100 and 48% at 1,000 tenants. 2I's entitlement keys do not cover the cost drivers (database size, BYOK, SSO, dedicated cell, PITR window), and trial limits conflict between 2H, 2I and OD-04. The consolidated registers follow: 38 unmade decisions, 66 assumptions, 59 numbered risks plus 3 delivery risks and 5 proposed new ones, after merging duplicates. No risk ID is reused for a different risk. The spine v2 change list gives replacement text for S0 to S8. The VC table maps each fact to its dependent items and a fallback, and proposes VC-13 to VC-22. Findings: 3 High, 14 Medium, 11 Low.

### 2.2 One cell-capacity rule (CR-1)

This text replaces A-PH-02, A-PH-03, A-NFR-09, J-7.2.3, J-7.2.4, J-SD2 and A-60's threshold clause. Exact edits are in P3-X3-01.

> **CR-1 Cell capacity.** For each shared cell, `T` is the placement threshold in tenant schemas. Warm-pool, canary, trial and `__r`/`__old` restore schemas all count toward `T`.
> - Until J-PERF has measured the knee `K` (J-7.2.2) on the production compute size, **`T` = 300 (provisional)**.
> - After measurement, `T` = floor_100(0.6 × `K`), and never above 0.6 × `K`. `T` is recorded in `control.tenant_cells` and in ADR-001's C1-05 follow-up, and re-measured under J-7.2.5.
> - **Secondary triggers.** Any one of these marks the cell `Full` regardless of count: `pg_class` rows above 250,000; database size above 60% of the tier's disk; p95 above A-NFR-03/04 for 3 consecutive days at the shared-cell compute ceiling (Large); a full migration rollout above 45 min (A-NFR-11); pooler clients above 70% of the limit at peak after a compute upgrade; backend resident memory above 60% of RAM (2J H3).
> - **Provision ahead.** A new cell is provisioned when a cell reaches 0.8 × `T`, or when any secondary trigger reaches 80% of its limit. This is an S3 digest alert (H-AL-03).
> - **Refuse at the threshold.** The control plane refuses to place a new tenant in a cell at or over `T`, or marked `Full`. Existing tenants stay.

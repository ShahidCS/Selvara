# Phase 3 review X1: coverage, data model vs API, control-plane placement, classification vs retention and encryption

Reviewer: X1. Scope: checks 1 to 4 of the X1 brief. Read-only review of `docs/architecture/backend-v2/phase2/*` and `adr/ADR-001..014`. Owner decisions (summary §8) and R2-01, R2-03 treated as binding.

## 1. Summary

Every module from M01 to M26 and every control-plane area has a home in 2A and 2B. Workstreams C, E, J and K reach almost all of them. The gaps are concentrated in four places.

1. **Reference-content upgrade has no API, no approval kind and no permission.** ADR-011 and 2B call for it; 2C, 2D and 2E leave it out. The M24 help desk is also missing from every release in 2K.
2. **The data model and the API disagree in about twenty places.** The main ones:
   - api-key scopes;
   - the webhook outbox status;
   - an exports table;
   - a Trust Center NDA signer name with no column;
   - account-API tour preferences stored in a tenant table;
   - nine control-plane tables named in 2C, 2E, 2F and 2I that 2B never defines.
3. **No tenant business data sits in the control plane, but four documents still contradict the binding reconciliation.**
   - ADR-010 still lists only S2 plus sessions and fails CI on any extra table. It also caches authorised sessions for 30 s, against R2-01, and gives `cp_rw` to every handler, against R2-03.
   - 2A A-CP-30 and 2C C-05 also give `cp_rw` to tenant-facing handlers, against R2-03.
   - 2D D-61 runs jobs from a 15-minute-old routing replica that ADR-010 says does not exist, against R2-01.
4. **Classification is the weakest area.**
   - 2B uses its own encryption codes (No/T/S/CP); 2F, ADR-003, 2G and 2K use E0/E1/E2.
   - 2B leaves most narrative free text in plaintext, while 2F and ADR-003 make it E1.
   - 2B places wrapped file keys in the tenant database, while 2F puts them in `ks`.
   - 2B's audit hash rule is the opposite of 2F's.
   - The control-plane platform key (CP) does not exist in 2F's key hierarchy.
   - Nine retention class names used in the documents are not defined.
   - 2B and the 2G retention schedule disagree on 14 periods.

Totals: 48 findings: 7 High, 26 Medium, 15 Low.

### 0.5 Elevation and approvals engine use (admin domain)
**Elevation (demo, `lib/elevation.ts`).** super/super2 only (`:33`); reason 15..300, ticket `TICKET_RE`, 1..60 minutes (`:50-67`); one at a time; dropped on role switch (`:94-97`); expiry computed from `until`. Audit (svc): `admin.elevated` (after "Elevated <n> min, until …; ticket: …; hardware key: simulated (demo); reason: …", categories Authentication, Account), `admin.elevation.ended`, `admin.elevation.expired` (`:62-65,106-109`). `assertElevated()` throws `SodError("Elevation required")` (`:86-88`); `ticketNote()` appends "; elevation ticket <T>" to billing and rates entries (`:92`).

**Two decision paths exist in the admin domain:**
1. `lib/adminApprovals.ts` (Billing, Rates): `createAdminApproval` requires super **and** elevation, sweeps expiry, refuses a second pending request with the same kind **and** subject ("A request for this item is already waiting for approval."), then `requestApproval({approverRoles:["super"], forbid:[requester], needs:1, domain:"admin", ticket})` with the engine's **default TTL 24 h** (`:17-22`). `decideAdminApproval` = super, elevated, kind prefix match, engine `whyCannotApprove`, reason ≥10 (`APPROVAL_REASON_MIN`, `:9`) stored ≤300, reject at once, or `precheck` then `approve` then `apply` (`:30-41`). Withdraw = super, elevated, requester only (`:43-47`).
2. `lib/approvalFlow.ts` (MFA resets): handler registry (`registerKind`, `:26`), `settle()` applies or closes on the next sweep (`:32-43`), adds the **named-approver** rule (`whyCannotDecide`, `:47-56`: with `needs` 1, only the named person may approve **or reject**), elevation for admin-domain decisions **and withdrawals** (`:62,84`), reason ≥10 (`:64`). Delegation never applies to admin-domain requests (`lib/delegation.ts:37`).

| Kind | Raised by | needs | approverRoles | forbid | TTL | Apply effect | Path |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `billing.credit`, `billing.refund`, `billing.markpaid` | `financeRequest` when USD value > 1,000 or unknown (`billing-admin.ts:121-136`) | 1 | super | requester | 24 h | `applyFinance` (1.3 B8) | adminApprovals |

### 1.7 Owner decision: internal ledger plus a payment provider (Stripe first, on only when its keys are set; `PRODUCTION_PROGRAMME.md:16`)
**What the code says the ledger must do** (`billing-admin.ts:1-5`, `services/billing.ts:1-3`): amounts as integer minor units; tax, totals and balance computed on the server; each invoice transition idempotent (client request id + state precondition) and audited in the same transaction; card data only through the provider's hosted fields; invoices generated on the server. The rates module adds: lock the rate in force into each invoice at issue (`rates.ts:5-7`, Rates rule list `RatesModule.tsx:70`).

**What the demonstration implies the ledger holds** (none of it exists on the server): per company a billing account (contact, address, tax id if any, currency, cycle, billing method, plan, add-on modules, seat and usage limits), price list versions (plans, add-ons, usage), invoices with lines, tax lines and the locked rate, a status machine (Draft? Issued/Open, Paid, Past due, Void, Written off), payments, credits, refunds and write-offs as separate ledger entries (today only `credited`/`refunded` counters), balance derived from entries, and the four-eyes requests of 0.5. ## 9. Gaps and oddities found (decide before porting)
- **Rates and billing are two copies**: billing reads the seed `RATES` (`billing-admin.ts:40`), Rates edits `RATE_ROWS`; no rate decision ever reaches billing, and GBP, INR, NGN finance requests always need a second approver.
- **Reject a held rate = release it** (`rates.ts:39,86-87`); no previous rate is stored to restore. A tax rate of 0 cannot be entered by edit (`rates.ts:62`).
- **Money is seed text**: tax not computed (Contoso 13.5 % against an 18 % rate row), Northwind "Annual" billed quarterly with the USD annual price written as AED, balance unrelated to unpaid invoices, MRR in USD list prices for non-USD customers and two seed MRRs matching no plan.
- **Ledger effects are partial**: credits not capped cumulatively; credit on a Paid invoice reduces the balance of other invoices; mark paid leaves the balance untouched when another invoice is past due; void leaves balance and Past due status unchanged; refunds move no balance; `credited`/`refunded` shown nowhere; a credit and a refund (or write-off) can be pending on one invoice at once (duplicate check per kind).
- **Void of a Paid invoice** is a single-person action with no refund and no second person.
- **"Issue & email", "Resend", "Retry", "PDF", "Export", "Refresh rates", "Run sample feed"** do nothing but log or toast; Export, PDF and Run sample feed are not even audited.
- **Workspace billing is unrelated to the platform's record** of the same company (different plan state, seats, invoices, amount); plan change is immediate despite "takes effect next billing cycle"; the denied page names owner, who cannot open the module; KPI caption "All paid" is literal; the payment page invites typing card display values.
- **Kit**: Billing, MFA and Platform security hand-build tables, a pager and `Kpi` tiles instead of `DataTable`/`KpiStrip`; Rates and workspace billing use the kit.
- **Wrong navigation for platform staff**: "Back to Overview" to `/ws/overview` on unknown billing ids; `ElevateGateBanner` sends support to Elevated Access, which support cannot open; the AppSec link on Platform security can never work for platform roles.
- **MFA module**: support can never withdraw its own request; reject is reserved to the named approver; ordinary customer users cannot be reset anywhere in the console; platform staff cannot be reset at all; effects are flags nobody reads; "notified at all contact points" sends nothing; seed request recreated with a fresh 24 h expiry per tab; seed people "Support, T. Ahmed" and "N. Hasan" are not personas; `mfa.*` entries map to RoPA PA-01 although PA-17 names MFA resets.
- **Approval visibility**: billing/rates requests appear only on their pages (not in the bell); nothing tells a requester about a decision or expiry; MFA bell items go to support, who cannot act.

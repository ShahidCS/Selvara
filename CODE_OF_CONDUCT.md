# Code of conduct

Be respectful and professional. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and sharing other people's private information are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, reviews or any other project space.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that break these rules, and may restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns to {{CONDUCT_CONTACT}}. Reports are handled confidentially. Security vulnerabilities are a separate process: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
